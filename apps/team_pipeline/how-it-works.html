Team Pipeline

Three agents process synthetic support tickets: intake routes and hands off, specialists resolve, and a reviewer wakes on the event stream to approve or correct. Corrections become GLOBAL team lessons. A fresh specialist recalls them across runs.

Measured: the cold pipeline resolves 1 of 4 held-out tickets. With team memory, 4 of 4.

handoff · feedback · events (SSE) · global lessonsAll tickets and agents are fictional

Cold defaults are wrong for refund-duplicate and rollback cases. The team lessons must come from the reviewer.

2 · Loop

Handoff

Intake writes a structured handoff entry: findings, open question, next step. Not a transcript dump. Each agent owns its run.

Event wake

The reviewer listens on the control event stream. A context.handoff_created event wakes it. It never polls. Live trace: 1 event seen per handoff.

Verdict

The environment holds the correct resolution. A wrong specialist decision earns a request_changes verdict.

Team lesson

The correction is written as a GLOBAL lesson. The server overlays global lessons into every future run — including the other lane's agents.

Apply

In fresh processes, specialists recall the team lesson and cite it when it decides the case. Evaluation writes nothing.
