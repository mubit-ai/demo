Librarian

A fleet writes messy memory: six restatements of one guidance line and four scattered vendor facts. While the system is idle, sleep-time consolidation distills the scatter into consolidated observations — and the canonical answer survives. Cold artifacts archive out and come back by ID.

Measured: 6 scattered entries in, 4 distilled observations out, recall quality intact, 2 of 2 artifacts recovered by dereference.

remember · memory_health · archive · dereferenceConsolidation runs while the run is idle

1 · Loop

Seed

Write 6 near-duplicate guidance entries and 4 scattered vendor facts. Snapshot the counts.

Idle

The consolidation worker sweeps the idle run. It distills scattered facts into consolidated observations. Raw evidence is not deleted.

Verify

Recall the canonical answer after the sweep. It must survive.

Archive + dereference

Cold exports move to the archive tier. dereference brings each back by ID — forgetting here is reversible.
